OneClick Folder

OneClick Folder is a small Windows tool. One click creates a new folder in your own Google Drive, turns on link sharing for that folder, and copies the link to your clipboard.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

OneClick Folder requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. It can access only the folders it creates. It cannot read your other Drive files.

The app talks only to Google. It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party, and it does not collect analytics.

The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your own PC and is used only to create folders when you run the app. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

To remove access, revoke OneClick Folder at your Google Account permissions page and delete the DriveOneClick folder in %LOCALAPPDATA%.
